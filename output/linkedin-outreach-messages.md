## Usage Notes

### When to use which template:

- **Template 1**: Stord-specific roles (Forward Deployed, AI Enablement)
- **Template 2**: Airtable-specific roles (Solutions Architect, Consulting)
- **Template 3**: General AI Solutions Architect / Forward Deployed roles at other companies
- **Template 4**: n8n community posts, automation builder roles

### Best practices:

1. **Personalize each message**: Research the person's background and mention specific connection points
2. **Timing**: Send connection requests within 24 hours of applying, follow-up 2-3 days after acceptance
3. **Follow-up cadence**: Initial message → 3-4 days → 1 week → stop (don't be pushy)
4. **Proof**: Always link to concrete evidence (GitHub, portfolio, case studies)
5. **Metrics**: Lead with numbers (90%, 170 hrs, 7,700 stars) — they're memorable and credible
6. **Call-to-action**: Always end with specific ask (15-min chat, case study discussion)

### Tracking outreach:

Add to your applications tracker:
- Date of connection request
- Date of follow-up
- Response received (yes/no/maybe)
- Meeting scheduled (date/time)
- Outcome
